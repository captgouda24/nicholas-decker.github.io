Beyond the Canson Case

Ms. Canson's surplus is not the only money that court filings say went missing around attorney Donald Iwuchukwu and his frequent co-counsel Metu Ogike. Lawsuits and State Bar complaints from at least nine households describe foreclosure surpluses that never reached the owner, "loans" that were never secured or repaid, and client trust funds held back. The amounts alleged come to more than $2 million.

What authorities have done

  • Since March 2025, Ms. Armstrong has filed reports with the Contra Costa DA (No. 013-1139031), the Los Angeles and Orange County DAs, the FTC (No. 192145463), the FBI, the U.S. Department of Justice, the OCC, the California Attorney General and the State Bar. She says the responses have called these civil matters for now.
  • Ms. Armstrong says the State Bar has previously closed complaints from Ms. Brin, Mr. Choe, Mr. Mooney, Ms. Kim, Ms. Oh and herself.
  • Two courts have ruled against the group on the merits so far: the 2017 restitution order in the Mooney matter, and the October 2025 denial of their anti-SLAPP motions in the Canson civil case.
